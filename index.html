요약. 이 게임에는 회원가입·로그인·광고·인앱결제가 없습니다. 게임 기록은 이용자의 기기에만 저장됩니다. 「온라인 방」으로 친구와 함께 플레이할 때에만 같은 방에 있는 사람들에게 보여 줄 별명과 진행 상황이 서버에 전송되며, 그 자료는 방을 나가면 즉시, 늦어도 24시간 안에 자동으로 삭제됩니다.

AZOTA GAMES(이하 「개발자」)는 「개인정보 보호법」 제30조에 따라 이용자의 개인정보를 보호하고 이와 관련한 고충을 신속하고 원활하게 처리할 수 있도록 다음과 같이 개인정보처리방침을 수립·공개합니다. 이 방침은 모바일 게임 엘리멘탈 체인(이하 「이 게임」)에 적용됩니다.

제1조 (처리하는 개인정보의 항목 및 수집 방법)

1. 기기에만 저장되며 수집하지 않는 정보

다음 정보는 이용자의 기기 저장소(localStorage)에만 기록되며 개발자에게 전송되지 않고, 개발자는 이를 열람할 수 없습니다.

  • 게임 진행 정보 — 도달 층, 점수, 최고 기록, 보유 코인, 저장 지점
  • 게임 설정 — 선택한 직업, 배경음·효과음 크기, 진동 세기, 표시 언어, 튜토리얼 열람 여부
  • 상점에서 구매한 게임 내 아이템 보유 내역
  • 이용자가 입력한 별명

앱을 삭제하면 위 정보도 함께 삭제됩니다.

2. 「온라인 방」 이용 시에만 수집·처리되는 정보

다음 정보는 이용자가 「멀티 캠페인 → 온라인 방」에서 직접 방을 만들거나 참여 코드를 입력한 경우에 한하여 수집·처리됩니다. 혼자 플레이하는 동안에는 게임 기록이 기기 밖으로 전송되지 않습니다.

다만 온라인 방 기능을 위해 Firebase 소프트웨어 개발 도구(SDK)가 앱에 포함되어 있으며, 이 도구는 이용자가 방을 이용하지 않더라도 앱 실행 시 수탁자(Google)의 서버에 연결하여 자체 초기화를 수행할 수 있습니다. 이는 개발자가 지시하는 처리가 아니며, 이 과정에 이용자의 별명이나 게임 기록은 포함되지 않습니다.

이 게임은 다음 정보를 일절 수집하지 않습니다.

이 개인정보처리방침은 시행일로부터 적용됩니다. 법령·정책 또는 서비스의 변경에 따라 내용이 추가·삭제·수정되는 경우에는 변경되는 개인정보처리방침을 시행일 7일 전부터 이 페이지를 통해 공지합니다. 다만 수집하는 개인정보의 항목이나 이용 목적이 변경되는 등 이용자 권리에 중대한 변경이 발생하는 경우에는 최소 30일 전에 공지하고, 필요한 경우 별도의 동의를 받습니다.

Summary. This game has no sign-up, no login, no ads and no in-app purchases. Your game data is stored on your device. Only when you play with a friend through an “Online room” is a nickname and your progress sent to a server so the other players in that room can see it, and that data is deleted as soon as you leave the room — and in any case within 24 hours.

AZOTA GAMES (“the Developer”) establishes and discloses this Privacy Policy in accordance with Article 30 of the Personal Information Protection Act of the Republic of Korea, in order to protect users' personal information and to handle related concerns promptly. This policy applies to the mobile game Elemental Chain (“the Game”).

Article 1 — Categories of personal information processed

1. Held on your device only; not collected

The following is written only to your device's storage (localStorage). It is not transmitted to the Developer, and the Developer cannot read it.

  • Progress — floor reached, score, best record, coins held, save point
  • Settings — chosen class, music and effects volume, haptics strength, display language, whether the tutorial has been seen
  • Items bought in the in-game shop
  • The nickname you entered

Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

2. Processed only when you use an “Online room”

The following is processed only where the user creates a room or enters a join code under “Play with others → Online room”. During solo play, no game data leaves the device.

The Firebase software development kit (SDK) is nonetheless bundled in the app for the online room feature, and it may connect to the processor's (Google's) servers to initialise itself when the app starts, even if the user never opens a room. This is not processing directed by the Developer, and neither the user's nickname nor any game data is included in it.

The Game has no chat feature. The only signals exchanged in a room are “start” and “progress update”; there is no path by which free-form text written by a user is transmitted.

3. Not collected

The Game never collects:

  • Identity information such as name, date of birth, phone number or e-mail address (there is no sign-up feature)
  • Device identifiers such as the advertising ID (ADID/GAID) or Android ID
  • Location, contacts, photos and videos, audio, files, or calendar
  • Payment information or purchase history (there are no in-app purchases)

The Developer processes personal information only for the purposes below. Should the purpose change, the Developer will take the necessary measures, including obtaining separate consent under Article 18 of the Personal Information Protection Act.

Personal information is retained only for the periods below and is then destroyed automatically and without delay. No request procedure is required.

Article 4 — Provision to third parties

The Developer does not provide users' personal information to third parties. Personal information is never sold or transferred for advertising purposes.

The display of a nickname and progress to other participants in the same room results from the user's own act of creating a room or entering a join code, is inherent to delivering the requested feature, and does not constitute provision to a third party.

Article 8 — Destruction of personal information

Personal information is destroyed without delay once the retention period elapses or the purpose of processing is achieved.

  • Procedure — the system deletes the data automatically at the points set out in Article 3, with no separate review step.
  • Method — personal information stored in electronic form is permanently deleted from the database in a manner that prevents recovery or reconstruction. No personal information is held in printed form.

Article 9 — Security measures

  • Encryption in transit — all communication between the device and the server is encrypted with TLS (HTTPS).
  • Restricted access — the database is locked by default, and security rules permit only authenticated users to access it and only to write their own entry. No user can alter or delete another user's entry.
  • Data minimisation — only what the online room feature requires is processed; device identifiers and advertising identifiers are not collected.
  • Minimal retention — data is destroyed automatically once its purpose is met, and within 24 hours at the latest.
  • Input validation — the format and size of stored values, such as nickname length, are constrained by server-side rules.

Article 10 — Behavioural information and advertising

The Game collects no behavioural information and serves no personalised advertising. It contains no advertising SDK, and the advertising identifier permission (AD_ID) has been removed from the application manifest so that such collection is not technically possible.

For reference, on Android you can delete your advertising ID or turn off personalised advertising under Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Article 11 — Children

The Developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 14 (the threshold under Korean law). The Game requires no sign-up and does not collect age information.

The only personal value transmitted to an online room is the nickname the user types. Guardians are therefore asked to ensure that a child does not enter a real name, school name or contact details as a nickname. If the Developer becomes aware that personal information of a child under 14 has been collected without the consent of a legal guardian, it will destroy that information without delay. Please use the contact in Article 12 to report such a case.
